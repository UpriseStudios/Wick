Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy explains what information Wax Bandits (the "game") collects, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. The game is made by Uprise Studios ("we", "us"), which is the data controller for the information described here. We are based in the United Kingdom.

Contact: {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}

The short version

  • You don't create an account or give us your name, email or phone number to play.
  • The game gives you an anonymous player ID so your vaults, progress and purchases are kept on our servers.
  • Ads are optional: you only see one when you choose to watch it for a reward. Ads are only personalised if you are 18 or over and agree to it.
  • We never sell your information.

What stays on your device

Vault drafts you haven't published, your settings, and raid clips you make stay on your device. A clip or vault link only leaves your device when you choose to share it, and then it goes to the app or person you pick. Our vault link pages show a vault's code, level and trap count, not who owns it.

Who we share information with

We use these service providers, which process information for us or as independent controllers under their own policies:

  • Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services: player ID, game data storage, server code, configuration and analytics; and Unity LevelPlay for rewarded ads). Unity privacy policy
  • Ad networks that LevelPlay uses to fill the ads you choose to watch, which may include Unity Ads, ironSource Ads and Google AdMob. The current list is in the consent screen the game shows you.
  • Google (Google Play billing and purchase checks, the User Messaging Platform consent screen, and the advertising ID). Google privacy policy
  • Apple (App Store purchases, on iOS). Apple privacy policy
  • Cloudflare (hosts our website and vault link pages, and passes ad reward confirmations to our servers). Cloudflare privacy policy

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect players and the game from fraud and abuse.

Some of these providers process data outside the UK and EEA, including in the United States. Where they do, they use safeguards recognised by UK and EU law, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, Standard Contractual Clauses or the UK-US data bridge.

Your choices

  • Ads: ads only play when you tap to watch one. Where your region gives you the choice (including the UK and EEA), you can change your ad consent choices at any time from the Privacy screen on the game's home screen. If you are under 18 or don't consent, any ads you watch are not personalised. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Analytics: only collected if you consent, and you can withdraw consent in the same place.
  • iOS: if we ever ask to track you across other companies' apps, it will be through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, and you can say no.

How long we keep it

We keep your game data for as long as you play, and delete it when you ask us to (see below). Purchase records are kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires (in the UK, six years). Analytics data and server logs are kept by Unity and Cloudflare for the periods set in their own policies, and are not used to identify you beyond your player ID.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your information, object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent at any time. To make a request, email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} with your player ID, which is shown on the game's Privacy screen. Because the game has no account, the player ID is how we find your data, so please include it. We will reply within one month.

Deleting the game from your device does not delete the data on our servers; email us to have it deleted.

If you are in the UK, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). In the EU, you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Wax Bandits is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Players under 18 never receive personalised ads. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections. Game data is stored by Unity Gaming Services, and only our server code can change your wallet, inventory and published vaults.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change is significant (for example, a new kind of data or a new purpose), we will tell you in the game before it takes effect.
