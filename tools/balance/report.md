Vaults are filled to most of their build budget with every defence their level unlocks, each at the highest upgrade level the Vault Level allows. The raider is a heuristic bot (a decent player, not a perfect one) with the best Wick upgrades for that level. Rerun after any change to the sim or the economy.

## Time to reach each Vault Level

Days from starting the game, raiding 15 times a day with the bot's average income at the player's own level. The free player watches the rewarded ads; the spender has the starter pack, Wick Club and the £19.99 gem pack every month, and spends gems finishing Vault Level builds. Defence and Wick upgrades aren't bought, so these are the fastest each could level. Target (game spec): the spender at most about 2x faster.
